2026-08-31 open-loop motor bench test

Scope
- Arduino Uno + one TMC2209 + one NEMA 17 stepper.
- No mux, no encoder, no linkage, no reducer.
- Firmware: motor_test, uploaded and verified on COM3.
- Microstepping: 8x from joint_config.h.

Wiring verified by behavior
- STEP path works.
- DIR path works.
- EN path works.
- Motor coil pairing supports smooth bidirectional motion.
- Common ground and motor supply wiring are stable under motion.

Power setup
- Bench PSU: 12 V.
- PSU current limit: about 1 A.
- PSU remained stable in CV during motion.
- Bulk capacitor installed across VMOT/GND at the driver.

Driver current setup
- Vref measurement point unresolved; no Vref pad on board.
- Meter reading near 4.33 V did not behave like the actual Vref wiper.
- Pot direction was identified empirically: tightening the screw increased usable current; backing it out lowered torque margin.
- Final setting: just above the threshold where 1600 steps/s stopped stuttering.

Firmware commands verified
- e toggles enable.
- f jogs +25 steps.
- r jogs -25 steps.
- Positive numeric jogs work.
- Negative numeric jogs work after firmware fix.
- c runs continuous forward.
- v runs continuous reverse.
- x stops continuous motion.

Motion results
- Positive steps rotated the bare motor counterclockwise when viewed from the shaft side.
- Negative steps rotated the opposite direction.
- Multi-thousand-step moves passed.
- 900 steps/s: smooth.
- 1066 steps/s: smooth.
- 1600 steps/s: initially stuttered, then ran smoothly after tightening the pot.
- 1600 steps/s at 8x microstepping = 1 motor rev/s = 60 rpm.
- Slight chugging remains audible, but no skipped steps or harsh stutter after adjustment.

Thermal result
- Driver remained cold.
- Motor remained cold.

Conclusion
PASS: first open-loop motor bench test complete. Proceed to reducer/linkage CAD and simulation workflow.
